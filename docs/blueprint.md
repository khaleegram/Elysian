# **App Name**: ElysianAI

## Core Features:

- Online Booking and Auto-Confirmation: Allow guests to book rooms online and receive instant confirmation. Auto-generate access pass (QR/PIN)
- Automated ID Verification & Fraud Scoring: Use AI to verify guest identities and score booking risks to prevent fraud, incorporating transparent reasoning as a tool to identify potential issues.
- Digital Check-In: Enable guests to check-in online and receive a digital room assignment.
- Guest Portal: Provide a unified portal for room service, housekeeping requests, and in-room services.
- Real-Time Request Tracking: Automate the ordering, scheduling, and real-time tracking of guest requests.
- Smart Notifications: Provide smart notifications for request status updates.
- Live Room Status Overview: Provide a dashboard with a live overview of room statuses and guest lists and AI anomaly detection with explainable alerts.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft lavender (#E6E6FA), reminiscent of tranquility and luxury, setting a calming tone.
- Background color: Very light, desaturated lavender (#F5F5FF) to ensure legibility and a serene backdrop.
- Accent color: Light purple (#D8BFD8) to complement the lavender, highlighting interactive elements.
- Font: 'Alegreya', a serif font known for its elegant, intellectual, contemporary feel; used for both headlines and body text.
- Use minimalist and elegant icons to represent different services and features.
- Clean and intuitive layout for both guest and staff interfaces, ensuring ease of use.
- Subtle transitions and animations to enhance user experience without being distracting.
